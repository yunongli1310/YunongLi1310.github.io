About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Pure Mathematics at the University of Waterloo, working in number theory under the supervision of Professor Kevin Hare.

Originally from Beijing, China, I attended the High School Affiliated with Renmin University before beginning my undergraduate studies at UC Davis. I later transferred to the University of Toronto, where I graduated in 2024 with a Mathematics Specialist and a Computer Science Major.

I completed my master’s degree in mathematics at the University of Waterloo in 2025. After graduation, I spent a year teaching high school mathematics at the Haidian campus of Beanstalk International Bilingual School in Beijing before returning to Waterloo to begin my Ph.D. in 2026.
